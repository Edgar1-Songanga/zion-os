# ZION Giving

Mobile-first giving flow: choose a fund, enter an amount, select a payment method, review, submit, and track confirmation. A receipt must only be issued after treasury confirms funds received. Bank transfer can be offered only when verified church banking details are configured. Multicaixa Express must stay unavailable until a real authorized provider integration and credentials are configured.

Suggested funds: Tithe, Church offering, Construction and building, Missions and evangelism, Sabbath School, Youth ministry, Children's ministry, Family ministry, Women's ministry, Men's ministry, Education, Welfare and community assistance, Music and worship, Media and communications, Local church projects, Conference/mission projects, Other designated fund.
