## 10. Adventist Identity & Spiritual Canon

ZION OS is explicitly designed with an Adventist spiritual identity. This identity is structural, not merely visual.

The Spiritual Layer must preserve and organize, with appropriate source/version metadata:

- The 28 Fundamental Beliefs of the Seventh-day Adventist Church
- Official church statements and declarations
- The Seventh-day Adventist Church Manual
- The Three Angels' Messages of Revelation 14
- Sabbath School lessons and study cycles
- Bible study and scripture references
- Devotion, prayer, spiritual growth and ministry resources

### Canonical Content Principle

Canonical Adventist material must be treated separately from:

- user-generated content;
- pastoral interpretation;
- educational commentary;
- community discussion;
- AI-generated explanations.

The system must preserve source, edition/version, publication date where applicable, language, rights/licensing metadata, and provenance.

### Adventist Knowledge Graph

The long-term Spiritual Graph should connect:

Bible passage
→ Fundamental Belief
→ Church statement/manual reference
→ Three Angels' Messages theme
→ Sabbath School lesson
→ devotion/study
→ sermon/ministry content
→ community discussion.

This graph should support discovery, contextual Bible study, spiritual education and responsible AI responses without presenting generated interpretation as official church doctrine.

### Sabbath School

Sabbath School is a first-class ZION capability, not a generic course module.

The architecture should support:

- lesson quarters/cycles;
- lesson number and title;
- daily study sections;
- Bible references;
- memory verse;
- discussion questions;
- teacher resources where licensed;
- class/group organization;
- study progress;
- notes and personal reflections;
- church/community discussion;
- multilingual editions;
- historical lesson archives where legally available.

### Institutional Authority

Where content represents an official Adventist position, ZION must distinguish the authoritative source from commentary and community material. Versioning and provenance are mandatory for canonical content.
